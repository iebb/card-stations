# Remaining northern and eastern Chinese station research — 2026-10-28

Reviewed every one of the 97 previously unreferenced lookup rows in 16 assigned city CSVs. Added 72 verified OSM references; 25 rows remain without a reference. All lookup codes and native labels are preserved.

Station nodes are used where supported. Tram and bus records with separate directional platforms use an explicitly verified parent stop area and its OSM bounding centre; this represents the station group, not one platform side. Existing interpolated tram coordinates are replaced only after the station identity is established. Cross-city rail services can share an OSM identity and reuse its reviewed coordinates. No card-code digits or station-code order establish a public line or physical station.

Former names, engineering names and station subtitles are individually documented below. Qingdao Line 2 uses the final July 2026 approval separately from the March proposal; proposed 上流佳苑 became approved 李家上流. References to construction or unopened stations establish physical identity, not operating service. Spelling-only 蒌葑 / 娄葑 remains unresolved.

Coordinates and translations come from the linked OSM object. The source-published Suzhou group declaration supplies two missing `7号线` labels; the exact Wikipedia station article supplies `Qunli`. No other non-OSM coordinates or generated translations are added. Public source URLs and field-level attribution are in the [compact evidence file](../research/remaining-china-north-2026-10-28.json).

## Validation

The evidence contains only public station facts and source links. Raw service responses, private card/report data and local diagnostics are excluded. OSM data remains subject to its [ODbL attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright); Wikipedia is used for cited factual station histories and published names.
